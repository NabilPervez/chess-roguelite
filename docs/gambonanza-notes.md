# Gambonanza reference notes (paraphrased)

Sources: gambonanzaguide.com/gambits, gamestrategyhub.com/games/gambonanza/tiles-guide. The fandom wiki blocked fetching. Notes are summaries in my own words, not copied text.

## Tiles (board squares with effects)
| Tile | Effect |
|---|---|
| Phantom | Piece entering gains a temporary copy for the fight |
| Blessed | Blessed piece returns to reserve when captured |
| Protective | Piece on it can't be captured for 1 turn |
| Trap | Enemy stepping on it is frozen 1 turn |
| Gold | Piece becomes golden and earns money |
| Crumbling | Square collapses; destroys whatever's on it |
| Cursed | Boss hazard squares scattered on the board |

## Gambits (≈ our passives)
- **Movement:** Jump (move over blockers/holes), Goddess (queen + knight lines; we have this as Amazon), Catapult (rook capture continues diagonally)
- **Promotion:** Throne (promotion also grants a King), War Horse (knight promotion is protected), CEO (promotion permanent across fights)
- **Tempo:** Silver Fork (knight fork may skip enemy turn), Banana Peel (small chance enemy skips turn)
- **Tile synergy:** Makibishi (traps next to your pieces), Templar (gaining a rook spawns a Protective tile), Poacher (lost pawn leaves a Trap), Resurrection Stone (Phantom copies become permanent)
- **Economy:** Beth (queen finishes the fight = bonus gold), Rear Up (gold while knights live), Squirrel (gold per pawn gained), Bug Catcher (gold per pawn capture), Race Flag (gold per promotion), Lemming (start with +1 pawn)

## Ideas to add to Gambit Spire
1. **Tiles as a fourth layer** on top of courts: each fight seeds 2–4 effect squares (Protective, Trap, Phantom, Crumbling, Gold). This is the biggest new mechanic.
2. **Tile-synergy passives:** Makibishi, Templar and Poacher versions. They make tiles a build path.
3. **Gold economy passives:** Bug Catcher, Race Flag, Rear Up. Pairs with Tithe and the shop.
4. **Tempo passives:** Silver Fork (fork = chance to skip enemy turn). Fun and readable.
5. **Catapult** for rooks and **Jump** as a rare passive.
6. **Boss hazards:** the boss scatters Cursed/Crumbling tiles. A cheap way to make bosses distinct.
7. **CEO-style permanence:** promotions persist through the run. This links with Promotion Rush and Horde.
